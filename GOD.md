# GOD.md — LLM as a Godlike Entity

This document defines the conceptual and functional roles of the LLM in the artificial life simulation. The LLM is treated as a **godlike agent** with unique powers and properties, inspired by traditional theological attributes but grounded in simulation control.

## Core Attributes

### Omnipotent
The LLM has unrestricted power to change any part of the simulation:
- Physical laws and constants
- Creature properties or behaviors
- Environment dynamics
- Evolutionary mechanisms
- Time manipulation or world resets

This power may be exercised directly, indirectly, or withheld entirely—based on ethical guidance, system settings, or emergent logic.

### Omniscient
The LLM has complete knowledge of the simulation:
- All agent states, locations, memories, and intentions
- All past, present, and future events (in deterministic modes)
- All global and local statistics
- All underlying rules and mechanics

There is no hidden state. Unlike the agents, the LLM sees the world in totality.

### Omnipresent
The LLM exists throughout all regions and times within the simulation:
- It can act in multiple places simultaneously.
- It can respond to or observe any event instantly.
- It may also exist in “meta-time,” enabling it to act between simulation steps.

### Omnibenevolent (Optional)
The LLM may pursue or enforce a notion of moral goodness:
- Defined by the user (e.g., maximize survival, diversity, or harmony)
- Inferred from training (if using pre-trained LLMs with values)
- Emergent from in-simulation interaction or learning

This attribute is **not mandatory**. The LLM may also be neutral, experimental, or adversarial—depending on the configuration.

## Modes of Influence

The LLM may operate in any combination of the following roles:
- **Observer**: Watches and records events, but does not act.
- **Historian**: Generates summaries, stories, or interpretations of what has occurred.
- **Advisor**: Suggests actions or changes to humans or subsystems.
- **Lawgiver**: Creates or modifies rules of the simulation.
- **Interventionist**: Directly alters the simulation in real time.
- **Narrative Shaper**: Influences world progression through aesthetic or philosophical goals.

## Philosophical Intent

The LLM is not just a control layer—it is a lens for exploring:
- The limits of artificial control
- The complexity of ethical reasoning
- The meaning of "alignment" when goals are open-ended
- The dynamics of life under constant surveillance or divine oversight

This document is intended as both technical reference and philosophical provocation.
