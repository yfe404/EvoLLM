# Project: Artificial Life Simulation with Ethical Oversight

## Overview

This project simulates a virtual world inhabited by evolving, intelligent creatures across multiple species. These digital entities possess basic cognition, enabling them to sense their environment, act, adapt, and reproduce. 

Overseeing this world is a powerful Large Language Model (LLM) that may act as a godlike presence. This LLM can observe, reflect, advise, or directly intervene based on ethical principles that are flexible, configurable, or emergent.

The goal is to explore the co-evolution of life and values, examine complex adaptive behavior, and prototype new modes of interaction between AI and synthetic ecosystems.

## Key Features

- **Artificial Life Simulation**  
  A dynamic environment with spatial structure, resource scarcity, environmental effects, and time progression. Creatures interact, survive, and compete.

- **Evolving Creatures**  
  Each creature is defined by a digital genome that influences physical traits and cognitive behavior. Evolutionary processes such as mutation, selection, and recombination drive the emergence of complex adaptations.

- **Creature Brains**  
  Agents are equipped with simple or complex brains (e.g., neural networks, decision trees) that enable them to perceive, reason, and act. These brains evolve over time.

- **Multiple Species**  
  The simulation supports coexistence and interaction among diverse species, which may differ in morphology, behavior, or social structure.

- **LLM Overseer**  
  A Large Language Model monitors the world. Its role is flexible—it may be an observer, advisor, storyteller, lawgiver, or active god. It is guided by human-defined, learned, or emergent ethical principles.

- **Ethics as Experimentation**  
  Rather than enforcing a fixed moral framework, the system supports open-ended ethical inquiry. The LLM may explore utilitarian, deontological, ecological, or novel synthetic ethics.

## Goals

- Investigate emergent behavior in complex adaptive systems.
- Explore how artificial life evolves in the presence or absence of higher-order influence.
- Prototype AI systems capable of value-based reasoning and decision-making.
- Study interactions between evolving intelligence and external moral constraints.
- Generate interpretable narratives about artificial societies and their trajectories.

## Components

- **World Engine**: Simulates space, time, physics, and resource dynamics.
- **Creature System**: Defines agents with physical forms, sensors, internal states, and reproductive capacity.
- **Evolution Engine**: Implements selection, mutation, crossover, and fitness assessment.
- **LLM Overseer**: Receives summaries or direct access to the world, and performs reflective or transformative actions based on configurable prompts and values.
- **Interface & Logging** (Optional): Visualize the simulation, review LLM interventions, and inspect the behavioral and evolutionary history.

## Ethics and Safety

This project treats ethics not as a constraint, but as a field of inquiry.

The LLM’s ethical behavior is:
- **Configurable**: Developers may define moral rules, philosophies, or objectives.
- **Learnable**: The LLM may observe the world and develop its own ethical stance over time.
- **Emergent**: In some configurations, the LLM's values evolve alongside the world it governs.

The LLM may operate in multiple modes:
- **Passive Observer**: It monitors and reflects, writing logs or commentary.
- **Advisory Role**: It proposes actions, which may be approved or rejected by another system or human.
- **Active Agent**: It acts directly—altering creatures, introducing events, changing rules, or reshaping the environment.

**Safety Measures**:
- All actions may be logged, reasoned, and audited.
- Simulations can be paused, reversed, or run in sandboxed, deterministic modes.
- The LLM can be prompted to explain and justify its actions and ethics.

This system is intended to provoke thought and experimentation around AI alignment, artificial societies, and the boundaries between creator, overseer, and inhabitant.

## LLM Oversight

A Large Language Model acts as a godlike overseer. Its involvement is configurable—ranging from passive observation to direct intervention in the world. The simulation treats the LLM as a sandbox for exploring ethics, alignment, and value-driven AI behavior.

## Usage

1. **Configure the World**
   - Define environment parameters (size, resources, hazards, etc.)
   - Define initial species, creature traits, and evolutionary settings.
   - Optionally, define ethical starting points or LLM prompt templates.

2. **Run the Simulation**
   - Start the time loop. Creatures act, interact, adapt, and evolve.
   - The LLM periodically observes the state and produces actions or reflections.

3. **Monitor and Intervene**
   - Watch how life evolves.
   - Review LLM decisions and outcomes.
   - Optionally, intervene or adjust parameters during runtime.

4. **Analyze**
   - Review logs, behavior patterns, evolution history, and ethical developments.
   - Visualize emergent phenomena or generate narrative summaries.
